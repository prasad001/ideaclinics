Ads Call Dashboard

Calls are the main goal for Google Ads and leads for Meta Ads. Compare month on month, by campaign and by category.

How the report is read

The page reads an Excel file laid out like IC_HYD_Report.xlsx. Upload an updated copy each month and it replaces the saved one.

  • A sheet with Google in its name: a Month table (Cost, Impressions, Clicks, Phone Calls, Received, Missed, From Ads, From Website), a Performance block (Missed and Received by Ad and Website), and a Row Labels block with each campaign by month.
  • A sheet with Meta in its name: a table by objective with a row per month (AdSpent, Impressions, Reach, Clicks, Leads), and a Month table with Amount spent, Impressions, Reach, Link clicks and Viewers.
  • Week wise needs dated rows, so it stays off until a daily export is added.
